# Breaking Into Wall Street - Financial Modeling Training

## About Breaking Into Wall Street

Breaking Into Wall Street is a structured training program for people who want to build real financial modeling skills. The Breaking Into Wall Street financial modeling path covers Excel mechanics, accounting, valuation, and deal analysis in a practical order. Instead of memorizing formulas, learners work through company models, build schedules, and connect statements the way analysts do on the job. Breaking Into Wall Street investment banking material focuses on the workflows that matter in live deals: spreading financials, projecting cash flows, and stress-testing assumptions.

The program is built for students, career changers, and working professionals who need a clear path from basics to advanced modeling. A Breaking Into Wall Street course review often highlights the same strengths: step-by-step videos, downloadable templates, and case studies that mirror real transactions. Breaking Into Wall Street private equity modules extend the same logic into buy-side analysis, including LBO models and returns attribution. Breaking Into Wall Street valuation lessons tie everything back to the core question: what is this business worth, and why?
